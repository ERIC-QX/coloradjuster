How the sliders change the CMYK

Hue turns the colourful part of the recipe around the C→M→Y ink wheel. Yellow turned + gains a little cyan; navy turned + loses a little magenta.

Saturation scales the colourful part and leaves the grey part (the amount C, M and Y share) and K alone.

Lightness scales all four inks: +10 means 10% less of each ink, −10 means 10% more.

These are exact, repeatable ink changes, not colour predictions. Photoshop converts through RGB and an ICC profile, so the same slider there gives different numbers. Print with AJET CMYK Through out, press, measure, and enter the readings here.
